## Phase 1: Discovery (Day 1 - Morning)

### What Happens
- Client fills out the detailed intake form
- Our team reviews requirements
- We align on brand identity, goals, and target audience
- Key requirements and must-haves are documented

### What We Gather
- **Brand Information**
  - Company name and tagline
  - Brand colors and style preferences
  - Logo and existing assets
  - Tone and voice guidelines

- **Business Goals**
  - Primary purpose of the website
  - Target audience definition
  - Key conversion goals (leads, sales, signups)
  - Success metrics

- **Technical Requirements**
  - Page structure (home, about, services, contact, etc.)
  - Special features needed (forms, booking, e-commerce)
  - Third-party integrations (CRM, analytics, payment)
  - SEO and performance requirements

- **Content Details**
  - Text content (if provided)
  - Images and media assets
  - Call-to-action preferences
  - Social media links

### Client Action Required
✅ Complete intake form with detailed information
✅ Provide brand assets (logo, colors, images)
✅ Share content or content outline
✅ List any must-have features

### Timeline
**4-6 hours** - Form review and requirements confirmation

### Output
- Requirements document
- Confirmed project scope
- Timeline confirmation

- **Modern Aesthetic**: Current design trends
- **Accessibility**: Readable and usable for all
