# Lenquant — Calendly Discovery Call Script

**Format:** 30-minute video or phone call
**Goal:** Understand the prospect's needs, show the 3 platform-generated site previews, close or schedule a follow-up

---

## Before the Call (Prep — 10 min)

- [ ] Pull up the prospect's existing website (if any) in a browser tab
- [ ] Open the Lenquant platform and confirm the 3 generated previews are ready for this lead
- [ ] Review any info they submitted at booking (company name, industry, notes)
- [ ] Have the pricing doc open for reference
- [ ] Screen-share ready

---

## 0:00–0:03 — Warm Open

> "Hey [Name], thanks for making time — really appreciate it. I'm [Your Name] from Lenquant.
> Quick heads-up on the agenda: I want to spend the first half understanding your business and what you're going for with your website, and then in the second half I'll show you something we actually built for you — three design directions for your new site. Sound good?"

**Wait for confirmation before moving on.**

---

## 0:03–0:15 — Discovery (Questions)

Work through these naturally — not a checklist, a conversation. You don't need to ask all of them.

**Business & Goals**
- "Tell me about the business — what do you do and who do you typically serve?"
- "What's the main thing you want the website to do for you? More leads? More credibility? Something else?"
- "Do you have an existing site? What do you like about it and what's frustrating you?"

**Design & Brand**
- "Do you have a logo or any brand colors we should match?"
- "In terms of vibe — modern and minimal, bold and energetic, warm and friendly — what direction feels right for your brand?"
- "Any sites you've seen that you think look great, even outside your industry?"

**Content & Pages**
- "Beyond a main landing page, do you need any other pages? About, services, portfolio, booking — anything like that?"
- "Do you have copy (text) written, or would you like us to write it based on what you tell us today?"

**Technical**
- "Do you have hosting and a domain already, or is that something we'd help you figure out?"
- "Any special functionality you need — booking calendar, e-commerce, integrations?"

**Timeline & Decision**
- "Is there a particular deadline you're working toward?"
- "Is it just you making this call, or do you have a partner or co-founder involved?"

**Take notes.** Anything they mention about pain points, goals, or aesthetics feeds into your close.

---

## 0:15–0:25 — The Big Show (3 Previews)

> "Okay — so this is actually something we do before every call. Based on your business, we generated three design directions for what your new site could look like. Let me share my screen."

**[Share screen — open the 3 previews in the platform]**

### Show Preview 1
> "This first one goes for a [clean/modern/bold — describe the direction]. You can see we pulled [color/tone/style cue from their brand]. The headline here is focused on [their core value prop based on what they said]."

**Pause. Let them react. Ask:**
> "What do you think? What stands out — good or bad?"

### Show Preview 2
> "This second direction takes a different approach — more [contrast: if 1 was minimal, 2 is bold, etc.]. Same business, different energy."

**Ask:**
> "Does this feel closer, further away, or is there something you like from both?"

### Show Preview 3
> "And the third option — this one [describe]. We included [specific feature: different layout, different hero, etc.]."

**Synthesizing question:**
> "If you had to pick a direction to go, which one is closest? And what would you want to change about it?"

**Key message to deliver naturally:**
> "What you're seeing is a real preview of what we'd build — not a mockup that disappears. Your final site is built to the same quality, with full production code you own forever. We deliver in 3 business days from the kickoff call."

---

## 0:25–0:28 — Pricing & Scope

> "So here's how it works. The base package is $1,000 — that covers your custom landing page, everything you saw, mobile-optimized, SEO setup, contact form, and 7 days of support after delivery. You own all the code."

**Then scope what they told you:**
- If they need extra pages: "You mentioned you'd want an About and Services page — those are $50 each, so that'd bring it to $1,100."
- If they need advanced features: "The booking integration you mentioned — that falls under our Advanced Features add-on, which is a one-time $300."
- If they need hosting: "If you want us to handle hosting too, that's $200/month — SSL, backups, everything managed. No contracts."
- If they want ongoing updates: "Our Maintenance plan is $500/month — 3 updates per month and a monthly call with us."

**State their total clearly:**
> "So for everything you described — [list items] — you're looking at $X one-time and $X/month if you go with [hosting/maintenance]. Does that feel in range?"

**Do not apologize for the price. Do not over-explain. State it clearly and wait.**

---

## 0:28–0:30 — Close or Next Step

### If they're ready to move forward:
> "Great — I'll send you a payment link right now. Once payment is confirmed, we'll lock in your kickoff call and your site will be delivered within 3 business days. You also have a 100% money-back guarantee within 7 days of delivery, so there's zero risk."

### If they want to think about it:
> "Totally understand. What I'll do is send you a summary of everything we discussed — the options, the pricing, and these three previews — so you can review it. What's a good timeline for a decision? I can follow up [day/date]."

> "One thing I'd say — the sooner we start, the sooner you have a site that's working for your business. The 3-day turnaround means you could be live by [date 3 days from now]."

### If they need to loop in someone else:
> "Who would need to be part of that conversation? Could we get them on a quick 15-minute call this week so I can walk through the previews with both of you?"

### If price is the objection:
> "I hear you. Just to give you some context — a freelancer typically takes 4–6 weeks and charges $2,000–$5,000+. An agency starts at $10,000. We're at $1,000 with a 3-day delivery and a money-back guarantee. The risk is completely on our side."

---

## Post-Call Actions (within 2 hours)

- [ ] Send follow-up email with:
  - Summary of what they said they need
  - Links to the 3 previews (or screenshots)
  - Pricing breakdown specific to their scope
  - Payment link (Stripe)
  - Calendly link for kickoff call
- [ ] Log notes in the platform (lead status → Contacted)
- [ ] If no response in 48 hours → follow-up message
