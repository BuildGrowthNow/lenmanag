# Lenquant — Discovery Call: Questions & Answers

This document covers every question you should ask the prospect during the Calendly call, plus common questions they will ask you and the answers to give.

---

## Part 1: Questions to Ask the Prospect

### Business & Goals

1. **Tell me about your business — what do you do and who do you serve?**
   - Listen for: industry, target customer, whether they're B2B or B2C

2. **What's the #1 goal for this website?**
   - Options to prompt: get more leads, sell products, look credible to investors, show a portfolio, book appointments

3. **Do you have an existing website? If so, what's the URL?**
   - If yes: "What's working about it? What isn't?"
   - If no: "How are you currently getting customers online?"

4. **Who are your main competitors? Can you share a couple of URLs?**
   - Helps calibrate the design bar for their industry

5. **What does your best customer look like — who are you trying to attract with this site?**
   - Age range, job title, business size, location if relevant

---

### Design & Brand

6. **Do you have a logo? Brand colors? Existing brand guidelines?**
   - If yes: ask them to share files before the build starts
   - If no: "That's fine — we'll build something that fits your business style"

7. **What's the visual vibe you want? (Modern & clean / Bold & energetic / Elegant & premium / Friendly & approachable)**
   - Show examples from the work showcase if helpful or from AWWWards

8. **Are there any websites — in your industry or outside it — whose design you admire?**
   - Even 1–2 URLs helps our designer calibrate

9. **Any colors or styles you definitely want to avoid?**

---

### Content & Pages

10. **What pages do you need? (Landing page only / About / Services / Portfolio / Blog / Testimonials / Contact / Booking)**
    - Reminder: landing page is included, each additional page is $50

11. **Do you have copy written (headlines, descriptions, service details) or should we write it based on your business info?**
    - Our team will craft the copy from the discovery call and any notes provided

12. **Do you have photos, product images, or a team headshot we can use?**
    - If no: "We use high-quality stock that matches your brand — or we can advise on a quick photo shoot"

---

### Technical & Hosting

13. **Do you already have hosting or a domain registered?**
    - If yes: we'll deploy to it; we can help with DNS setup
    - If no: "We offer managed hosting for $200/month — SSL, backups, scaling all handled for you — or we can recommend a DIY option"

14. **Do you need any special functionality? (Booking calendar, e-commerce, newsletter signup, API integrations, membership login, AI, chatbot, support, edit)**
    - If yes → likely triggers the $300 Advanced Features add-on

15. **Is there a specific deadline you're working toward?**
    - Reassure: standard delivery is 3 business days from the kickoff call

---

### Budget & Decision

16. **Have you worked with a web agency before? If so, how was that experience?**
    - Surfaces concerns to address (past bad experiences, price anchoring)

17. **Who else is involved in making this decision — is it just you or do you need to loop in a partner/co-founder?**
    - Important for closing — get all decision-makers on the call if possible - objective to close on the call

18. **What's your timeline for getting started?**

## Part 2: Questions the Prospect Will Ask You (and How to Answer)

### About the Offer

**Q: What exactly do I get for $1,000?**
A: A fully custom-designed landing page built for your specific business — not a template. It's mobile-responsive, SEO-ready, has a lead capture form, and you own all the code and assets. We deliver in 3 days and give you a 100% money-back guarantee.

**Q: Is this a template or custom-built?**
A: Custom-built. We start from scratch for every client based on your brand, industry, and goals. You can see examples of our work at [sites.lenquant.com].

**Q: What if I need more than one page?**
A: Each additional page is $50. So if you need a landing page plus an About and Services page, that's $1,000 + $100 = $1,100. We scope everything on this call so there are no surprises.

**Q: Do you write the copy or do I?**
A: We handle the copy. Based on what you tell us about your business today, we write headlines, descriptions, and CTAs. You review everything and we revise until you're happy.

---

### About Delivery

**Q: How do you actually deliver in 3 days?**
A: We've built a streamlined process over 6+ years. The discovery call gives us everything we need — from there our design and development team works in parallel. Day 1 is design, Day 2 is development, Day 3 is QA and delivery.

**Q: What if I want changes after it's delivered?**
A: You get 7 days of post-delivery support included. Any revisions in that window are covered. After that, our Maintenance plan ($500/month) covers 3 updates per month plus a monthly strategy call.

**Q: What format is the final deliverable?**
A: We hand off the full source code (HTML/CSS/JS or React depending on your needs), all assets, and deployment instructions. You own it completely — no lock-in.

---

### About Hosting & Maintenance

**Q: Do I need your hosting?**
A: No. You can host it anywhere — Vercel, Netlify, your existing server. We'll help you get it deployed. If you want a zero-hassle option, our managed hosting is $200/month and includes SSL, automatic backups, and scaling.

**Q: What does the maintenance plan actually cover?**
A: $500/month gets you a 1-hour monthly strategy meeting, 3 content or design updates per month, ongoing performance monitoring, and security patches. It's for clients who want a hands-off experience after launch.

**Q: Can I cancel the monthly services?**
A: Yes — no contracts, cancel anytime.

---

### About Trust & Guarantee

**Q: What if I don't like the result?**
A: We offer a 100% money-back guarantee within 7 days of delivery. If you're not satisfied for any reason, we refund the full amount. Zero risk.

**Q: How many clients have you worked with?**
A: Over 100 clients across industries like real estate, fitness, consulting, legal, restaurants, and more. You can see real examples and testimonials on our website.

**Q: Do you have experience in my industry?**
A: We've built sites for [reference relevant industries from the portfolio]. Bring up specific case studies if the prospect's industry matches.

---

### About the 3 Options We Show

**Q: Why are you showing me 3 versions of my site?**
A: We built a preview of what your new website could look like before you even commit. Each version takes a different design direction — so you can see the range and tell us which feels right for your brand. It gives you confidence in what you're buying before any money changes hands.

**Q: These look great — are these the actual deliverables?**
A: These are high-fidelity previews. Your final site is built with full production code — same design quality, built to be live and fully functional.

**Q: Can I mix and match elements from the 3 options?**
A: Yes — just tell us what you like from each and we'll blend them into your final design during the build.
