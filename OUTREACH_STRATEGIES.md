## Strategy 1: 🎯 The Preview Showcase (Cold Email)
**Best for:** Mid-market companies with outdated websites | High intent | Decision makers

### The Hook
Send an initial email with a PREVIEW of their redesigned website embedded or linked. Show them the dramatic before/after transformation of their current site redesigned by our AI.

### Email Script

```
Subject: [Company Name] redesigned in 3 hours

Hi [First Name],

I spent this morning redesigning [Company Name]'s website—just to show you what's possible.

➜ Current site: [their URL]
➜ What it could look like: [preview link to redesigned version]

We specialize in turning outdated websites into conversion machines. 3 days. $1,000. No meetings.

Your current site has:
❌ [Specific issue from analysis]
❌ [Mobile/conversion issue]
❌ [Speed/SEO issue]

Most companies lose 40% of visitors before they even scroll.

Want to see what a proper redesign could do for you?

[Preview Link] — Takes 60 seconds

Best,
[Your Name]
P.S. If this isn't a fit right now, that's totally fine. Send it to whoever handles your digital presence.
```

### Decision Tree

```
INITIAL EMAIL SENT
│
├─ YES (Opens email + clicks preview)
│  │
│  ├─ IMPRESSED by preview?
│  │  ├─ YES → SEND: "Quick question" follow-up with success story
│  │  │       (Builds on momentum, specific results data)
│  │  │
│  │  └─ NO → SEND: "One more option" email with different angle
│  │           (Alternative approach, focus on speed/cost)
│  │
│  └─ CLICKS MULTIPLE TIMES?
│     └─ YES → COLD CALL after 24h (warmed prospect)
│
└─ NO (No opens after 72h)
   └─ SEND: Subject line test "Quick question about [Company Name]"
      (More personal, less salesy)
      └─ After 5 days → MOVE TO STRATEGY 2
```

## Strategy 2: 💼 The LinkedIn Value Ladder
**Best for:** C-suite & decision makers | LinkedIn active | Content-oriented

### The Approach
Build authority through LinkedIn before asking. Position yourself as a website transformation expert.

### Phase 1: Value Posts (Week 1)
Post 2x per week showing:
- Before/after website transformations
- Website design mistakes that kill conversions
- Industry-specific insights (e.g., "Why most construction company websites fail")

### Phase 2: Strategic Connection
After 5-7 posts, connect with prospects with personalized message:

```
LinkedIn DM (First Connection)

Hi [Name],

I've been following [Company Name] and noticed something about your site 
that I think you'd want to know.

Most companies in your industry have the same design problem—it kills conversions. 
I've helped [Similar Company] fix theirs.

Curious if it's something you've dealt with?

[Your Name]
```

### Phase 3: If They Accept
```
LinkedIn DM (After Connection Accepted)

Hey [Name], appreciate you accepting!

Quick question: Is your website generating the leads you want?

The reason I ask: I noticed [specific observation about their site].

We just redesigned a similar company in your space—took 3 days, cost $1K, 
and increased form submissions by 34%.

Worth a quick look?

[Preview Link] + [Success Story]
```

### Decision Tree

```
CONNECTED ON LINKEDIN

├─ ACCEPTED CONNECTION?
│  │
│  ├─ YES → SEND PHASE 3 MESSAGE
│  │       │
│  │       ├─ REPLIES (ANY MESSAGE)?

│  │       │

│  │       │  └─ SEND: Value post link + "thought of you"

   └─ Send ONE more message: "Saw this and thought of you" + article
```

## Strategy 3: ☎️ The Direct Call (Warm or Cold)
**Best for:** Enterprise | High-ticket opportunities | Personal touch needed

### Call Script

**Objective:** Get 90 seconds of their time to discuss their site and show preview

```
[Prospect picks up]

"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Lenquant. 
I know you're busy—I'll keep this to 90 seconds.

I was looking at [Company Name]'s site and thought you'd want to know:
[One specific observation about their current site].

This is costing you leads.

Here's what's interesting: we built a redesign mockup in about 30 minutes 
to show what's possible. 

Would it be worth a quick look? Takes 60 seconds."

[Wait for response]

IF YES:
"Perfect. Here's the link: [preview URL]
Look it over, and I'll send you a quick message after with details.
When could we talk—tomorrow or Friday?"

IF "TELL ME MORE NOW":
"Sure. We do custom website redesigns in 3 days for $1,000. 
No long process, no meetings. We analyze your current site, 
design something that converts better, and launch it.

Would seeing what that looks like be worth a quick call?"

IF "NOT INTERESTED":
"Totally fair. One question though: when was your site last redesigned?"
[Listen, use their answer as intel]
"Got it. If you ever want to revisit it, reach out. I'll leave 
the preview link in an email after this. Take care!"
```

### Decision Tree

```
CALL ATTEMPT
│
├─ PROSPECT ANSWERS
│  │
│  ├─ OPEN TO TALKING (Positive signals)?
│  │  ├─ YES → SEND PREVIEW LINK
│  │  │       └─ FOLLOW-UP EMAIL 2h LATER
│  │  │          ├─ OPENS & CLICKS? → SEND: Quick proposal
│  │  │          └─ NO ACTION (48h)? → SEND: Value-add article
│  │  │
│  │  ├─ "SEND ME SOMETHING" → SEND: Email with preview + 1-pager
│  │  │  └─ After 3 days: RE-ENGAGE with "Any thoughts?"
│  │  │
│  │  └─ SAYS "NOT NOW" → SEND: Calendar link for 30-day check-in
│  │     └─ DO NOT CALL AGAIN (use email only)
│  │
│  └─ DEFENSIVE/RUSHED
│     └─ SEND: Short email recap + preview
│        └─ Use email follow-up strategy only
│
├─ CALL WENT TO VOICEMAIL
│  │
│  └─ SEND: Voicemail summary email
│     ├─ PROSPECT CALLS BACK? → Run call script
│     ├─ NO RESPONSE (3 days)? → Email with preview + subject "Following up"
│     └─ STILL NOTHING (7 days)? → Move to Strategy 4
│
└─ CAN'T REACH (INVALID #, ALWAYS BUSY)
   └─ PIVOT TO EMAIL OR LINKEDIN
```

## Strategy 4: 📬 The Curiosity Email Sequence (Email)
**Best for:** Prospects who won't take calls | Ghosted prospects | B2B decision makers

### Email 1: The Data Hook
```
Subject: Your site is losing [X]% of visitors

[Name],

We analyzed [Company Name] against industry benchmarks.

Here's what we found:
• 68% of visitors leave on first impression (design issue)
• Your site takes 4.2 seconds to load (industry avg: 2.1s)
• Missing [specific conversion element]

This is $[estimated monthly revenue loss] per month in opportunity cost.

The good news? This is fixable.

[Preview Link] shows what a 3-day redesign looks like.

[Your Name]
```

### Email 2: Social Proof (If no response in 3 days)
```
Subject: [Industry] company just did this...

[Name],

We just redesigned a company similar to yours. 
They went from [metric] to [metric].

Curious what's possible for [Company Name]?

[Preview Link]

Also—if you're thinking "we're too busy for a redesign," 
that's exactly why we did this. 3 days. No meetings.

[Your Name]
```

### Email 3: Pattern Interrupt (If no response in 7 days)
```
Subject: Honest question—why is your site still that way?

[Name],

I get that you're busy. But I don't get why most companies 
leave money on the table for years with outdated sites.

Is it:
a) You're waiting for the perfect time (never comes)
b) Previous designers let you down
c) Didn't think it would make a big difference
d) Just not a priority right now

Either way, here's something to think about: 
[Success story with numbers]

When you're ready to revisit it:
[Preview Link]

[Your Name]
```

```
SEND EMAIL 1

│  └─ YES → SEND EMAIL 2 (social proof) after 3 days
│           ├─ ENGAGES AGAIN? → MOVE TO PROPOSAL
│           └─ NO? → Continue sequence
│
├─ OPEN BUT DON'T CLICK (24h)?
│  └─ SEND EMAIL 2 with different hook (social proof angle)
│
├─ NO OPEN (48h)?
│  └─ SEND EMAIL 3: Subject line test "Question for you"
```

## Strategy 5: 🎭 The Partnership Play (Email + LinkedIn)
**Best for:** Agencies, service providers, complementary businesses | Building relationships

### The Angle
Position as a referral partner, not a sales pitch. Build mutual benefit relationships.

### Email to Agency/Designer

```
Subject: Partnership idea—your clients might want this

Hi [Name],

I've been following [Agency Name]'s work and I'm impressed with 
your design quality.

Here's what I thought: most of your clients probably get redesigns 
every 3-5 years. Between projects, they're missing conversions.

What if you could offer them a quick refresh every year? 
We do high-quality redesigns in 3 days for $1K. 
You could white label it or refer directly—margin's built in either way.

Could be a nice revenue stream + keeps clients happier.

Worth a conversation?

[Your Name]
```

### LinkedIn Relationship Building

1. **Week 1:** Follow + engage with their posts (like + thoughtful comments)
2. **Week 2:** Share their work in your network (tag them)
3. **Week 3:** Send email
4. **Week 4:** If response → Schedule call to discuss partnership model

### Decision Tree

```
SEND PARTNERSHIP EMAIL
│
├─ POSITIVE RESPONSE?
│  │
│  ├─ WANTS TO DISCUSS → SCHEDULE CALL
│  │  └─ Call objective: Agree on 
│  │     • Revenue share or referral fee
│  │     • How they introduce it to clients
│  │     • First joint project
│  │
│  ├─ "SEND MORE INFO" → SEND: 1-pager + case study
│  │  └─ FOLLOW-UP after 5 days: "Thoughts?"
│  │
│  └─ CURIOUS BUT HESITANT → SEND: "How about we try one together?"
```

## 💡 Pro Tips

**Preview Mockup Creation:**
- Use your platform to generate a basic redesign of their site in 15 minutes
- Show 2-3 key improvements (design, speed, mobile)
- Include conversion rate lift estimate
- Make it look professional but acknowledge it's a preview

**Email Subject Lines (Proven Winners):**
- "[Company] redesigned in 3 hours"
- "Your site is losing [X]% of visitors"
- "Quick question about [Company]"
- "We analyzed your site. Here's what we found:"
- "Honest feedback on [Company]"

**Timing Matters:**
- Prospects check email: 8-10 AM, 5-6 PM
- Call prospects: Tuesday-Thursday, 10-11 AM or 2-3 PM
- LinkedIn messages: 9 AM or 6 PM
- Follow-ups: 3-5 days after initial contact

**Personalization = 3x Response Rate:**
- Mention something specific about their site
- Reference their industry challenges
- Use their first name
- Make it clear you've actually looked at them
